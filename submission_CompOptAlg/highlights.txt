Research Highlights:

- Formulates state-robust portfolio selection over a continuous family of kernel-weighted conditional return distributions.
- Establishes continuity, convexity, and existence properties of the resulting semi-infinite CVaR model.
- Solves a finite grid-restricted approximation using adaptive constraint generation and a full-grid separation oracle.
- Evaluates grid resolution, bandwidth, empirical support, transaction costs, and bootstrap sensitivity.
- Reports competitive long-run wealth but no statistically significant Sharpe-ratio advantage over standard benchmarks.
